The kick-off meeting brings together all consortium partners to:

•	Build a shared understanding of the approved project objectives, work packages, deliverables, milestones and expected impact.

•	Confirm consortium governance, partner responsibilities, reporting, financial management, quality assurance and risk controls.

•	Agree on the initial implementation plan, cross-work-package dependencies, measurable indicators and the first six-month action register.

•	Develop a practical shared approach to curriculum modernisation, capacity building, industry engagement and resilient internship systems.

•	Strengthen partner relationships through institutional visits, industry engagement and cultural activities on Days 4–5.


## Agenda

Day 1 | Monday, 18 January 2027
Theme: Consortium alignment, governance and project controls

Day 2 | Tuesday, 19 January 2027

Day 3 | Wednesday, 20 January 2027

Day 4 | Thursday, 21 January 2027
